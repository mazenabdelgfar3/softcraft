# Ordering Flow & WhatsApp Checkout — Softy Crafts

This store does NOT use traditional online checkout or payment gateways.
The final order must always be completed through WhatsApp.

## Pricing Rule
Products display starting prices:
- "يبدأ من XXX جنيه" (Starts from EGP XXX)

Communicates that the final price depends on selected customizations.

## CTA Button Rules
Primary CTA:
- "اطلبي عبر الواتساب" (Order via WhatsApp)

Secondary CTA:
- "تخصيص البوكيه" (Customize Bouquet)

DO NOT USE:
- Checkout, Proceed to Payment, Pay Now, الدفع الآن.

## Product Customization
Customers customize:
- Bouquet size (حجم البوكيه)
- Flower colors (ألوان الورد)
- Wrapping style (نوع التغليف)
- Greeting card message (رسالة كارت الإهداء)
- Delivery date (تاريخ التوصيل)
- Quantity (العدد)
- Special notes (ملاحظات إضافية)
