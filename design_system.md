# UI/UX Design System – Feminine Luxury Flower Store

Create a premium, elegant, feminine flower e-commerce website that is emotionally appealing to women. The experience should feel soft, luxurious, calm, and effortless. Every design decision should encourage browsing and increase purchase intent.

## Design Style
- Luxury Minimal
- Soft Feminine
- Modern Boutique
- Premium Floral Brand
- High-end Gift Shop

Avoid generic AI-looking layouts.
Avoid dark themes.
Avoid cluttered interfaces.
Prioritize whitespace and visual balance.

## Color Palette
- Background: #FFF9F7
- Surface: #FFFFFF

- Secondary: #D98FA5
- Accent Gold: #C8A96A

- Text Secondary: #7D7272
- Border: #F2E7E9

Colors must feel warm, elegant, and luxurious.

## Border Radius

- Buttons: 16px
- Inputs: 14px

## UX Rules
Keep every screen clean.
Maintain 35–45% whitespace.
Maximum 2 primary colors per screen.
No heavy borders.
Soft shadows only.
Use premium spacing.
Touch-friendly mobile design.
Responsive on every device.
Fast loading.
Optimize for conversion.

## Emotional Experience
The user should immediately feel:
Elegant
Warm
Romantic
Premium
Trustworthy

The website should inspire women to imagine gifting the bouquet before they even click "Add to Cart."

Overall goal:
Create a high-converting luxury floral shopping experience that feels like a premium boutique rather than a traditional online store.
